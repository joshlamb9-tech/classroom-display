Before You Go
The week before the trip
🛂
Passports — drop off to the school office
Parents need to hand your passport in to the school office before the trip. Make sure this happens — we cannot take you without it.
🧳
Packed ski bag — in school the last week
Your packed ski bag needs to be in school during the final week before departure so it can be weighed. Don't leave it until the morning of the trip.
🚗
Airport drop-off on Saturday?
If you're being dropped off at the airport, bring your bag with you — it doesn't need to come into school. You just need to make sure your passport has been handed in to the office beforehand.
Newcastle Airport
At the airport
🛂
Passports
Staff collect passports when we arrive. You'll get them back when you need them. Make sure yours is in your bag when you leave Mowden.
👥
Stay Together
We move through the airport as a group. You do not go anywhere alone — not to a shop, not to the toilet. Buddy system is on from the moment we arrive.
🔒
Security
Follow staff instructions through security. No liquids over 100ml in hand luggage. If in doubt, ask before you pack it.
🚪
At The Gate
We establish a base at the gate. That's where you come back to. Wait for instructions before boarding. Don't wander.
✈️
On The Plane
Follow the instructions of the cabin crew. Stay in your seat when told to. If you feel unwell, tell a member of staff immediately.
🤝
You Represent Mowden
Other people are trying to travel. Be polite, be patient, and be calm. The airport is not a playground.
Where We're Staying
Montgenèvre, French Alps
🏔️
The Resort
Montgenèvre sits on the French-Italian border at 1,850m. Part of the Milky Way ski area — over 400km of pistes.
🏨
Where We Stay
Village Club du Soleil — right in the heart of the resort. Room allocations decided by staff. We'll show you where the staff bedrooms are when we arrive — they'll have a sign so you always know where to find us.
❄️
The Snow
High altitude means reliable snow. Expect blue skies, big mountains, and long runs. Suncream is not optional.

Daily Routine
A typical ski day
Morning 🌅 Thermals and salopettes on — that's breakfast kit. Kit check tunnel before you head out. Suncream on. No exceptions.
Morning ski ⛷️ Morning session on the slopes with your ESF instructor.
Lunch 🍽️ Hot lunch back at the hotel.
Afternoon ski ⛷️ Two-hour afternoon session. Kit check again before you go. Back to the hotel before dark — register with your group.
Evening 🚿 Shower and change. Supper together. Then a different evening activity every night.
On The Slopes
Ski groups
How Groups Work
Your ski instructor will be chosen for you based on your skiing ability. Each group has a designated Mowden staff member to check in with.

Within your group, you operate in buddy pairs — you are always with someone.
ESF Instructors
All instructors hold full French ski qualifications. They know the mountain. Listen to them — what they say goes.
On The Mountain
  • ›Never ski off-piste or away from your group
  • ›Helmet and ski jacket on at all times
  • ›No GoPros or cameras on your helmet
  • ›Carry your emergency contact card at all times
  • ›If someone is hurt — stop, stay, tell your instructor
"It's your holiday as well as our holiday."
We all want the same thing — a brilliant week. That means looking out for each other and making good choices.
📵
No Electronics
No phones, tablets, or screens on the trip. The mountain is better than any screen. Be here, be present.
👫
Groups of 4 — But Not a Family of 4
You stay in groups of 4, within your larger Mowden group which checks in with a staff member. Being in a group of 4 doesn't mean you can go wherever you want.
🛏️
Rooms at Curfew
In your room, lights out on time. No moving between rooms after curfew. Staff check.
🏫
Represent Mowden
Other people are on their holidays too. Be kind, be polite, be grateful. You're an ambassador whether you like it or not.
☀️
Suncream Every Session
High altitude sun is no joke. SPF 50 on face and neck before every session — kit check will confirm it. No cream, no skiing.
💶
Spending Money
You get three hot meals a day — breakfast, lunch on the mountain, and supper. Spending money is for treats, souvenirs, or the odd hot chocolate. You don't need a lot.
Looking After Each Other
Medical & wellbeing
We are here for a good time. No one has to suffer in silence.
Communicate. Speak to a member of staff. Speak to your friends. Check in on each other.
European Emergency Number
112
Police, fire, ambulance. Works across France. Staff carry this. If something serious happens — shout for us first.
Medical Officer
Mrs Mackie
All medication and medical needs go through Mrs Mackie. If you feel ill at any point — day or night — come and find us.
Piste Patrol
Mountain Rescue
If someone is hurt on the mountain: stop, stay with them, and tell your instructor immediately. Do not move them.
